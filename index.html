Prognoser CRC risk classification and molecular annotation Prognoser-Web

A single expression matrix, a complete colorectal cancer molecular annotation.

Prognoser transforms a colorectal cancer expression profile into risk classification, microsatellite status, CMS / iCMS subtype, immune context, and multi-omics pathway activity without installing software or uploading patient data.

Fully client-side 18 analysis modules Open source

All calculations run locally in your browser. Your data never leaves this device.

01

Risk classification

ssGSEA features and a bundled random forest model assign each sample to iHRS or iLRS.

02

MSI status

A PreMSIm-style kNN classifier predicts MSI-H and MSI-L directly from expression.

03

Molecular views

CMS, iCMS, hallmarks, immune infiltration, immunophenoscore, and metabolic flux.

Methods

Reproducible, direction-aware scoring

Prognoser uses GSVA-style ssGSEA, validated random forest models, and direction-resolved signatures. Every comparison reports a statistical test and can be exported as CSV or PNG.
